What you get. A .doc file that Word, Pages and Google Docs open and edit normally: your text, paragraphs and headings. The original's exact typesetting — columns, absolute positions, embedded fonts — is not reproduced, and a scanned PDF has no text to recover (run it through Photo → Text first).
